# Glossary — ATA 21-10 Air Supply

## General Terms

- **Bleed Air:** Compressed air extracted from the engine compressor section for use in aircraft systems.
- **Precooler:** Heat exchanger that reduces bleed air temperature before delivery to downstream systems.
- **Pack:** Air conditioning pack (air cycle machine assembly) that conditions bleed air for cabin use.
- **Mass Flow:** The rate of air mass moving through the system, typically measured in kg/s or lb/min.

## Components

- **Bleed Valve:** Valve controlling the extraction of bleed air from the engine.
- **Pressure Regulator:** Device maintaining bleed air pressure within specified limits.
- **Isolation Valve:** Shutoff valve used to isolate bleed air supply in case of fault.
- **Check Valve:** One-way valve preventing reverse flow in the bleed system.
- **Flow Control Valve:** Valve regulating the amount of bleed air flow.

## Safety Terms

- **Overheat:** Condition where bleed air temperature exceeds safe operating limits.
- **Bleed Leak:** Unintended escape of bleed air from ducting or components.
- **Contamination:** Presence of unwanted substances (oil, hydraulic fluid, fumes) in bleed air.
- **Hot Air Leak Indication:** Warning system detecting high-temperature air leaks.

## Performance Terms

- **Duct Pressure Loss:** Reduction in air pressure due to flow through ducting.
- **Flow Capacity:** Maximum sustainable air mass flow rate through the system.
- **Pressure Ratio:** Ratio of inlet to outlet pressure across a component.
- **Thermal Margin:** Difference between operating temperature and material limit.

## Interfaces

- **APU Bleed:** Bleed air supplied from the Auxiliary Power Unit.
- **Crossbleed:** Connection allowing bleed air transfer between left and right systems.
- **Pack Inlet:** Entry point for bleed air into the air conditioning pack.

## Abbreviations

- **APU:** Auxiliary Power Unit
- **ECS:** Environmental Control System
- **FADEC:** Full Authority Digital Engine Control
- **HP:** High Pressure (compressor stage)
- **IP:** Intermediate Pressure (compressor stage)
- **OAT:** Outside Air Temperature
- **PRSOV:** Pressure Regulating and Shutoff Valve
